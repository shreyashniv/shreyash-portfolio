# about.py

Background

I'm a Data Scientist at a deeptech startup, working at the intersection of machine learning, statistical reasoning, and domain knowledge. My job isn't just to build models — it's to understand the problem space well enough to make models that are actually useful. That means spending as much time on domain analysis as on code.

My academic background is in MSc Data Science & Analytics at Brunel University London, where I developed a strong foundation in statistical modelling, ML theory, and applied analytics. I work primarily in Python — scikit-learn, PyTorch, and the broader data stack — but I'm equally comfortable in R for statistical work and SQL for anything data pipeline related.

What makes my work different is the domain layer. I don't treat data as abstract — I care about what it represents. Whether it's industrial sensor readings, healthcare waiting lists, financial risk signals, or sports performance metrics, I try to understand the domain first and let that shape the modelling decisions. Good data science is domain-aware data science.
